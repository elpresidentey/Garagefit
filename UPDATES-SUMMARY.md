## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Largest**: Hero titles (68-140px)
2. **Large**: Section headings (38-64px)
3. **Medium**: Body text (17-18px)
4. **Small**: Labels & notes (11-12px)

### Spacing System
- **Micro**: 4-8px for tight elements
- **Small**: 14-20px for related content
- **Medium**: 24-32px between sections
- **Large**: 52-88px for major sections
- **XL**: 120-180px section padding

### Black Brand Palette
- **Pure Black**: #0a0a0a (primary brand)
- **Dark Gray**: #1a1a1a (hover states)
- **Mid Gray**: #404040 (accents)
- **Light Gray**: #6b7280 (secondary text)
- **White**: #fff (contrast on dark)

### Animation Timing
- **Fast**: 0.3s (micro-interactions)
- **Medium**: 0.7-1.2s (transitions)
- **Slow**: 8-14s (Ken Burns effects)
- **Easing**: cubic-bezier(.22,.68,.22,.99)
